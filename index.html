      STEP 1

      Tap to answer

      Every answer saves the moment you tap it. Change your mind and tap again; it updates. The bars under each question show how the team answered so far.

      STEP 2

      Earn your tickets

      Each question you answer is one ticket, ten in total. Add your name at the bottom to enter. Five $10 Starbucks cards are drawn at the end of the month.

      STEP 3

      See what changed

      HR reads the results and the "You said, we did" box at the top shows what changed because of your answers.

      How your answers are handled

      • Your name is never shown next to your answers. Everyone sees totals only.
      • Your draw name is kept on a separate list that only HR can read.
      • Written answers about time sinks and safety, and your nominations, are only readable by HR, and the results page shows them without your name.
      • A site's pulse average only shows once five people from that site have answered.
